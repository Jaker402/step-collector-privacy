Step Collector Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

In short: Step Collector reads your steps (and floors climbed) from Apple Health so your walking can earn rewards in the game. Your daily step totals are stored with your game account so you and the friends in your groups can see them. We don't show ads, we don't sell or share your data, and Health data is never used for advertising.

Who we are

Step Collector is a walking game made by an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means the Step Collector team. You can contact us at StepCollectorGame@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your account: your email address and a password (stored securely by our login provider, never readable by us), and the username you choose.
  • Your steps: your total steps for each day, read from Apple Health (or, if Health isn't available, from your phone's motion sensor). Steps you type into the Health app by hand are left out.
  • Your game progress: your character's look and outfit, the items and crates you have, your scrap, level and XP, titles, achievements, daily challenges, the friend groups you join and the days you open the game.

Apple Health

With your permission, Step Collector reads two things from Apple Health:

  • Step count, including steps from an Apple Watch, to earn crates, experience and rewards, and to show your steps to the friends in your groups.
  • Flights climbed, shown only on your own Stats screen. These are read on your phone and are not stored by us.

Step Collector only reads from Apple Health; it never writes to it or changes anything there. Health data is not used for advertising or marketing, is not sold, and is not shared with anyone except as described in "Who can see your information" below. You can turn off Step Collector's access at any time in the Health app or in your iPhone's Settings (Privacy & Security, Health).

How we use your information

  • To run the game: counting your steps, giving crates and rewards, and saving your progress.
  • To show you and your friends the leaderboard and each other's characters.
  • To keep the game fair: unusual jumps in step counts are checked and may be capped.

We don't use your information for advertising, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and the game contains no ads or third-party analytics.

Who can see your information

  • Friends in your groups can see your username, title, character and outfit, level, daily and weekly step totals, Collection Score, Step Score and the medals you choose to show. People not in a group with you can't see any of it.
  • Our service provider: the game's data is stored with Supabase, which hosts our database and login in Sydney, Australia. Supabase stores the data for us and doesn't use it for its own purposes.

We don't sell your information and don't share it with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while you have an account. To delete your account and everything stored with it, email us at StepCollectorGame@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, and we'll delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the app doesn't delete your account.

Security

Your information is sent over encrypted connections, and our database only lets each player read what they're allowed to: your own data, and what your group mates can see as described above.

Children

Step Collector isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll let you know in the game.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: StepCollectorGame@gmail.com.
